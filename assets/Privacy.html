Privacy Policy for VoxSherpa

Effective Date: May 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VoxSherpa. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Android application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data Collection and Use

VoxSherpa is designed to respect user privacy. We do not directly collect, store, or share your personal identity data. You are not required to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personal information to use the core features of this app. However, we utilize third-party services for advertising and billing which may collect specific identifiers as outlined below.

3. Advertising and Advertising ID

To support the ongoing development of VoxSherpa, we display advertisements in the app. Third-party ad networks may collect and use your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and general usage data to provide personalized or non-personalized ads. You can manage or reset your Advertising ID directly from your Android device settings.

4. In-App Purchases and Billing

We offer in-app purchases to unlock premium features. All financial transactions are securely processed by Google Play. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card details or sensitive payment information on our servers. Google Play shares your purchase verification status with the app to activate your premium features.

5. Internet and Network Access

The app requires an internet connection solely for the following purposes:

  • Fetching AI Models: The app connects to our secure database (Google Firebase) to fetch the latest list of available AI voice models and download the necessary model files directly to your device.
  • Ads and Billing: To load advertisements from our ad network partners and to verify your purchase status through Google Play Services.

Once a model is downloaded, the text-to-speech (TTS) generation happens 100% offline on your device.

6. Local Storage

VoxSherpa requests storage permissions to save the downloaded AI models and the audio files (WAV, AAC) that you generate. These files remain locally on your device and are never uploaded to any external server by us.

7. Third-Party Services

The app utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, purely for functional, monetization, and security purposes.

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Firebase (used strictly for hosting and delivering AI model files)
  • Google AdMob (for displaying advertisements)
  • Google Play Billing Library (for processing in-app purchases)

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time as we add new features to VoxSherpa in future versions. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
